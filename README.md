# Privacy Policy — 13 Line Quran

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the Android app **13 Line Quran** (package `com.simpleislam.simple13linequranrecitation`), published by Simple Islam on Google Play.

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal or sensitive user data.**

## What the app does with data

- **No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.** The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.
- **Data that stays on your device:** your bookmarks, hifdh mistakes, last-read page, chosen reciter, display settings and any recitation audio you download are saved only on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently.
- **Recitation audio:** when you choose to play or download a recitation, the app fetches the audio directly from [everyayah.com](https://everyayah.com), or from full-length recordings on [archive.org](https://archive.org) (Qari Ayoob Essack) and [mp3quran.net](https://mp3quran.net) (Sheikh Badr al-Turki and several other reciters), over a secure (HTTPS) connection. As with any internet request, those servers can see your device's IP address. No other information about you is sent. Everything else in the app works without internet.
- **Permissions:** the app uses the Internet permission, only to fetch recitation audio. So that a recitation can carry on while the screen is off, it also runs as a media player with a notification (pause, next, previous and stop on the lock screen), and keeps the phone awake while a recitation is playing, and only then. None of these permissions gives the app access to any personal data.

## Sharing with third parties

We do not share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
